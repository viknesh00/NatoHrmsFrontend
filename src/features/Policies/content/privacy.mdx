# Privacy Policy

> **Review draft:** This page describes the HRMS data categories visible in the application. The organization operating this workspace must verify its legal identity, controller/processor roles, contact details, jurisdictions, retention schedule, and subprocessors before treating this as its formal privacy notice.

## Data used in this workspace

Depending on your role and the features enabled, the HRMS can process employee identity and contact details, job and department information, onboarding submissions and documents, attendance events and clock-in location, leave requests, work-status records, timesheet entries, and account or role information.

## Why the information is used

The application presents this information to support the HR workflows available in the workspace: employee administration, onboarding, attendance, leave, work-status reporting, timesheets, announcements, and role-based dashboards. Access to screens is limited by the roles and department rules configured for the application.

## Location information

When a user performs a clock action, the application may request browser location permission and submit location coordinates as part of that attendance workflow. Browser permission can be managed in the browser settings. The organization should explain its attendance-location policy and the operational consequences of declining permission.

## Access, retention, and sharing

HRMS screens use role-based visibility, but the employing organization remains responsible for defining who is authorized to view or change each category of employee data. The repository does not establish a complete retention schedule, legal basis, hosting region, subprocessor list, or cross-border transfer policy; the organization must publish those details through its approved privacy notice.

## Your requests

For questions or requests concerning access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights, contact the HR or privacy contact designated by your organization. Add the approved contact and response process here before external publication.
