# Terms of Use

> **Review draft:** These service-use guidelines are not a signed employment, subscription, or data-processing agreement. The organization operating the workspace must approve the final terms and identify the responsible legal entity before relying on them as contractual terms.

## Using the HRMS

Use the application only through the account and role assigned to you. Keep your sign-in details private, use only the records needed for your work, and report suspected account misuse or inaccurate access promptly to your administrator.

## Employee and HR records

Enter information accurately and use the HRMS for legitimate workforce processes. Do not access, export, disclose, or alter employee records outside your assigned responsibilities. Do not use another person's account or attempt to bypass role-based access controls.

## Attendance and location

Clock-in and clock-out actions are intended to record work attendance. Where the workflow requests browser location permission, use the feature in accordance with your organization's attendance and location policy. Do not submit another person's attendance event or falsify a work record.

## Availability and changes

The application supports internal HR workflows and may be maintained or updated by the service operator. Feature availability, support channels, service levels, data export, termination, and dispute terms must be supplied by the organization or service agreement; this draft does not define them.

## Questions

Contact your HRMS administrator or the organization-designated support channel for access, policy, or service questions.
