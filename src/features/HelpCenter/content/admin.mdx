## People and access

<details>
<summary>How do I add or update an employee?</summary>

Open **Employees** from the sidebar. Choose **Add Employee** to create a profile, or use the row action to edit one. Employee roles and reporting managers are set in the employee form.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I review organization activity?</summary>

Use the dashboard for organization KPIs, attendance, leave requests, projects, announcements, and events. The **Organization** page shows reporting relationships available to your account.

</details>

## Time and attendance

<details>
<summary>Why does clock-in require browser location?</summary>

Clock actions request a precise GPS fix from the browser. Allow location access in the browser and retry. If the device cannot provide an accurate fix, the clock action remains blocked.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I review or correct a timesheet?</summary>

Open **Timesheet** to review employee entries by month. Use the employee row to open its calendar. Editing rules depend on the selected month and approval state.

</details>

## Leave and company updates

<details>
<summary>Where are recent leave requests and approvals?</summary>

The dashboard’s **Recent Leave Requests** section summarizes requests. Choose **View All** to open the Leave module and review available actions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I manage company events and announcements?</summary>

Use **Calendar** for company events and holidays, and **Announcements** for company updates.

</details>
