## Onboarding tasks

<details>
<summary>Where do I submit employee details?</summary>

Open **Onboarding** from the sidebar and use the employee details form provided for your submission. Follow the form’s required fields and validation messages.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I check the status of my submission?</summary>

Open **Work Status** to view the status information available to your account. Contact your HR administrator if a submission needs correction or is missing.

</details>

## Account help

<details>
<summary>How do I update my details after submitting?</summary>

Use the edit action shown for your submission when available. If the action is unavailable, contact your HR administrator.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I cannot access a page?</summary>

Pages are shown according to your role and department access. Contact your HR administrator if you believe a page should be available to you.

</details>
