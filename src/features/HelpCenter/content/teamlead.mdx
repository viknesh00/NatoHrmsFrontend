## Team overview

<details>
<summary>What does the dashboard show me?</summary>

The dashboard provides team-level KPIs and the attendance, people, project, announcement, and event information available to your account.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I view the reporting structure?</summary>

Open **Organization** from the sidebar. Search for a person or department, then select a manager card to expand or collapse that branch.

</details>

## Work and time

<details>
<summary>Where do I add or update work status information?</summary>

Open **Work Status** from the sidebar. The actions available to you depend on your role and the current record state.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I review attendance or timesheets?</summary>

Use **Attendance** for attendance records. Open **Timesheet** to review employee timesheets available to your role and select a row to see its calendar.

</details>

## Leave and updates

<details>
<summary>Where can I find leave requests?</summary>

Use the dashboard’s **Recent Leave Requests** section or open **Leave** from the sidebar to review requests visible to you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where are holidays and announcements?</summary>

Open **Calendar** for company events and holidays, and **Announcements** for company updates.

</details>
