## Your team

<details>
<summary>Where can I see team and department metrics?</summary>

Open **Dashboard** for department KPIs, attendance, leave requests, projects, announcements, and upcoming events. Available data is scoped by the backend to your access.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I find reporting relationships?</summary>

Open **Organization** from the sidebar. Search by name, role, department, email, or employee ID. Select a manager card to expand or collapse the reporting branch.

</details>

## Attendance and timesheets

<details>
<summary>Where can I review attendance?</summary>

Use **Attendance** for attendance records and **Dashboard → Attendance** for the current summary. Filters and available records follow your role access.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I review an employee timesheet?</summary>

Open **Timesheet**, select the employee and month, then open their calendar. The current application applies its existing month and approval-state editing rules.

</details>

## Leave and updates

<details>
<summary>Where do I review leave requests?</summary>

Recent requests appear on the dashboard. Use **View All** or open **Leave** from the sidebar to see the requests available to you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find company events and announcements?</summary>

Open **Calendar** for holidays and events, or **Announcements** for company updates.

</details>
