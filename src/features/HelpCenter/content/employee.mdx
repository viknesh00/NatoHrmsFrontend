## Your profile

<details>
<summary>How do I view or update my profile?</summary>

Select your profile picture in the header to open **My Profile**. Use **Edit Profile** to update fields available to you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find my timesheet?</summary>

Open **Timesheet** from the sidebar. Select a date to add or review time details when editing is available.

</details>

## Attendance and leave

<details>
<summary>Why does Clock In ask for location?</summary>

Clock In and Clock Out use your browser’s location. Allow location access for this site and try again. If your account uses geofencing, turn on precise location so your device can report a location accurate to within 50 m.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I request leave?</summary>

Open **Leave** in the sidebar and choose the leave request action. Your submitted requests appear in the Leave module when available.

</details>

## Company information

<details>
<summary>Where can I find holidays and announcements?</summary>

Use **Calendar** for company holidays and events, and **Announcements** for company updates.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I contact HR about an issue?</summary>

Use the HR or support contact provided by your organization. This Help Center explains the application’s current screens and does not submit a support ticket.

</details>
